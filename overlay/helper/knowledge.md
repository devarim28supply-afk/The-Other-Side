# The Other Side - what the helper should know

Facts come from the game's own files (folder and asset names) and fan guides for version 0.9.8.x. Some details may be out of date; when unsure, say "maybe".

## The game
- 1-6 player co-op ghost hunting, like Phasmophobia but its own rules. The team goes to a haunted location, finds evidence, identifies the ghost type (Identify mode) or cleanses it (Cleanse mode), then leaves by the vehicle.
- There are about 25 ghost types (Ataphoi, Banshee, Bhoot, Demon, Doppleganger, Hupia, Iblis, Marid, Nas Nas, Phantom, Poltergeist, Puca, Revenant, Shura, Skia, Sluagh, Strigoi, Tantalus, Tariaksuq, The Echo, The Forgotten, Wewe Gombel, Wiederganger, Wisp, Wraith). Each has 3 evidences (harder difficulties add false evidence).
- The ghost's body/look can be: Bride, Clown, Hans, McGavin, Nun, Orphan child, Patient, Zombie, Zombie girl. Named story ghosts that speak lines: Hans, McGavin (Victoria story), Orphan, Skia, a female "Responses" ghost.
- Instead of sanity, players have a heart rate; anxiety meds (pills) calm it.
- There is a fuse box / breaker; the ghost can trip it (lights go out, CCTV cameras stop).
- Tarot cards: Death, The Fool, The Hanged Man, The Star (respawn / revive on easier difficulties).

## The 7 evidence types and how they show up
- EMF: EMF reader hits 20+ mG (fully red). Must be seen live.
- Orbs: only on infrared cameras (CCTV with breaker on, or wireless camcorder).
- UV: glowing hand/footprints or stains under a UV light or glowstick.
- Freezing: room temperature below 0 C / 32 F (thermometers). The coldest room is usually the ghost's favorite room.
- Audio: the ghost answers with clear phrases on the Spirit Box or Infrasound Receiver.
- Writing: the ghost writes or scribbles in the Blank Book or on the Drawing Easel. A writing / scribbling / page sound near the book is probably WRITING EVIDENCE, worth telling the player to go check the book.
- Radiation: Geiger counter ticks fast (over 1000 CPM / past the green bar).

## Hunts (most important)
- A hunt is when the ghost tries to kill players. A loud door SLAM often starts it.
- HEARTBEAT sound = the ghost is hunting / can see you. Louder heartbeat = closer. Tell the player to hide or run.
- During a hunt: hide in a closet (not behind boxes), break line of sight around corners, drop electronics. Holy Water spray stuns the ghost for about 3-5 seconds. Crosses and the Obsidian Shield Candle protect.
- Ghost footsteps getting louder during a hunt = coming closer. Getting quieter = moving away.
- After the heartbeat and hunting sounds stop for several seconds, the hunt is probably over.
- Bhoot: the room gets about 30 F colder about 3 seconds before it hunts.

## Ghost events (scripted scares that are NOT always a hunt)
Footsteps walking across a hall, a bang, a door slam, a whisper, piano or guitar playing, a rocking chair, lights bursting, a shadow appearing, the ghost appearing behind or in front of a player, crows, cabinets opening, a phone ringing, a table flipping. These mean the ghost is ACTIVE nearby; tell the player where.

## Ghost-specific sounds
- Banshee screams (also through the Spirit Box).
- Skia gives unusual, sympathetic Spirit Box answers.
- Sluagh can show four orbs at once.

## Equipment sounds the player may hear
Spirit box static and voices, Geiger counter clicks, EMF beeps (the KII EMF reader beeps), thermometer clicks, camcorder, motion sensor and trip wire sensor alarms (someone or the ghost passed), holy water spray, lighter / candle, phone (cellphone apps: camera, evidence guide, phantom detect, stopwatch, tasks, vitals).

## How to read the overlay's rows
- "(far)" = quiet, probably far away.
- Footsteps on one side while the player stands still are someone else: a teammate or the ghost. Footsteps alone are not proof of the ghost; footsteps plus heartbeat or hunting sounds are.
- "YOU:" captions are the player's own computer voice; other captions are teammates or ghost lines ("GHOST (...)").
- Sound names come from a small AI and can be wrong; a single odd sound means little, a pattern means more.
